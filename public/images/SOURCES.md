# Image provenance

## Life Luxe Travel's own photographs

Everything in this folder **except** the files listed below was taken
on a Life Luxe journey, by the people who were there. Those are the
photographs the gallery and the homepage scatter are built on, and
the claim made beside them ("no stock, no staging") depends on it.

Do not add stock to that set.
