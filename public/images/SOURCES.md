# Image provenance

## Life Luxe Travel's own photographs

Everything in this folder **except** the files listed below was taken
on a Life Luxe journey, by the people who were there. Those are the
photographs the gallery and the homepage scatter are built on, and
the claim made beside them ("no stock, no staging") depends on it.

Do not add stock to that set.

## The NYE 2027 campaign images

`nye-2027-dress-up.png` is the "Dress up. Show up. Celebrate." image
from the same campaign (`virgin-voyages-friends-no-fireworks.png`).
Like the hero, it is a rendered promotional image of models — the
folder it came from also holds `Gemini_Generated_Image_*` files — not
a photograph of a Life Luxe group. It is used to set the tone of a
sailing that has not happened yet, and it is never captioned as a
Life Luxe journey. Replace it with a real photograph from the 2027
voyage once there is one.

## The NYE 2027 band

`nye-2027-brilliant-lady.png` is Terry's own campaign asset — the
hero from lifeluxetravel.com/nye-2027-cruise, pulled from the GHL
media library (`brilliant-lady-luxury-realistic-hero.png`). It shows
Virgin Voyages' Brilliant Lady against the Miami skyline. Its file
name says "realistic", so treat it as a rendered or composited
promotional image rather than a documentary photograph; it is not
presented as a Life Luxe journey.

**If a real photograph of any of these trips arrives, replace the
stock with it.** The `dest-` prefix exists so these are easy to find.
